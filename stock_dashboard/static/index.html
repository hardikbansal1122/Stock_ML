Research Methodology

How signals are produced, logged, and evaluated. This platform is for research and paper trading only.

Objective

Signal Desk identifies NSE-listed equities with elevated probability of a favorable 5-day move, using a gradient-boosted classifier trained on technical features. Output is a ranked confidence score, not a trade recommendation.

Universe & data

Each scan processes a fixed liquid Nifty-aligned universe via Yahoo Finance (NSE/BSE suffixes). Six months of OHLCV history feed rolling technical indicators (returns, moving averages, RSI, volatility, volume ratios, Bollinger position).

Model & signal

An XGBoost binary classifier outputs P(target=1) per ticker. Signals above the user-defined confidence threshold are ranked and logged. Features align with the training pipeline (step1–step3 in this repository).

Paper trading protocol

  • Each scan batch is written to Excel with entry price and a target exit date.
  • After seven calendar days, Resolve Exits fetches live prices and records outcome and return.
  • Performance aggregates are maintained on the Performance sheet in the workbook.
Risk Framework
  • Never put more than 10% of capital in one stock
  • Always set stop-loss at -3% from entry price
  • Exit positions after 5 trading days regardless of P&L
  • Three consecutive losses → stop trading for the week
  • Paper trade for 4–6 weeks before using real money
  • This is a research tool, not a guarantee. Markets can break any model.

About Signal Desk

An institutional-style research terminal for systematic equity screening on Indian markets.

Platform

Signal Desk is a local research dashboard that connects a trained ML model to a daily NSE scan, Excel-based paper trading log, and signal history viewer. It is designed for disciplined experimentation—not discretionary hype or execution.

Scope & limitations

Results depend on data quality, liquidity, and regime. Backtests and paper logs do not include slippage, fees, or corporate actions unless you add them externally. Nothing on this site constitutes investment advice.

Stack

  • Python / Flask API, XGBoost, yfinance, openpyxl
